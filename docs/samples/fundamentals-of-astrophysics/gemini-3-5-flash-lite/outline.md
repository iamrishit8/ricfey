# Fundamentals of Astrophysics: A Working Guide

_Depth: working_

A 4-week practical course on understanding celestial mechanics, stellar evolution, and cosmic scales using accessible physics.

## Week 1: Scales and Starlight

**Day 1: Cosmic Distances**  
Topics: `distance-scales`, `inverse-square`

- Calculate light-years to parsecs
- Explain the inverse-square law for brightness

**Day 2: The Electromagnetic Spectrum in Space**  
Topics: `em-spectrum`, `photons`

- Identify which wavebands penetrate Earth's atmosphere
- Relate photon wavelength to energy

**Day 3: Blackbody Radiation**  
Topics: `blackbody`, `wiens-law`

- Apply Wien's Law to find a star's temperature
- Calculate peak wavelength from temperature

**Day 4: Stefan-Boltzmann Law**  
Topics: `stefan-boltzmann`, `luminosity`

- Calculate star luminosity from radius and temperature
- Explain surface flux differences

**Day 5: Stellar Spectroscopy**  
Topics: `spectroscopy`, `spectral-classes`

- Interpret absorption lines in stellar spectra
- Identify spectral classes O through M

## Week 2: Stellar Physics and Evolution

**Day 6: Hydrostatic Equilibrium**  
Topics: `hydrostatic-equilibrium`, `stellar-structure`

- Balance gravity and pressure in a simple stellar model
- Explain why stars do not collapse instantly

**Day 7: Nuclear Fusion in Cores**  
Topics: `nuclear-fusion`, `mass-energy`

- Describe the proton-proton chain
- Calculate mass-energy conversion using Einstein's equation

**Day 8: The Hertzsprung-Russell Diagram**  
Topics: `hr-diagram`, `stellar-evolution`

- Plot stars on an HR diagram
- Identify main sequence, giants, and white dwarfs

**Day 9: Low-Mass Star Evolution**  
Topics: `stellar-evolution`, `white-dwarfs`

- Trace the path of a Sun-like star off the main sequence
- Describe planetary nebula formation

**Day 10: High-Mass Stars and Supernovae**  
Topics: `supernovae`, `stellar-evolution`

- Describe core collapse in massive stars
- Differentiate Type Ia and Type II supernovae

## Week 3: Stellar Remnants and Gravity

**Day 11: White Dwarfs and Degeneracy**  
Topics: `white-dwarfs`, `chandrasekhar-limit`

- Explain electron degeneracy pressure
- State the Chandrasekhar limit

**Day 12: Neutron Stars and Pulsars**  
Topics: `neutron-stars`, `pulsars`

- Describe neutron star formation
- Explain the lighthouse model of pulsars

**Day 13: Introduction to Black Holes**  
Topics: `black-holes`, `schwarzschild-radius`

- Calculate the Schwarzschild radius for a given mass
- Define the event horizon

**Day 14: Orbital Mechanics Basics**  
Topics: `keplers-laws`, `orbital-mechanics`

- Apply Kepler's Third Law to binary systems
- Calculate orbital periods from masses and separation

**Day 15: Binary Star Systems**  
Topics: `binary-stars`, `mass-transfer`

- Determine masses using spectroscopic binaries
- Identify Roche lobes and mass transfer

## Week 4: Galaxies and Cosmology

**Day 16: Milky Way Structure**  
Topics: `milky-way`, `stellar-populations`

- Identify galactic disk, bulge, and halo components
- Describe stellar population differences

**Day 17: Galaxy Classification**  
Topics: `galaxy-classification`, `hubble-sequence`

- Classify galaxies using the Hubble tuning fork
- Distinguish spirals from ellipticals

**Day 18: Dark Matter Evidence**  
Topics: `dark-matter`, `rotation-curves`

- Interpret galaxy rotation curves
- Explain the necessity of dark matter

**Day 19: Cosmic Expansion**  
Topics: `hubbles-law`, `redshift`

- Calculate recession velocity using Hubble's Law
- Interpret cosmological redshift

**Day 20: The Early Universe**  
Topics: `cmb`, `big-bang`

- Describe the Cosmic Microwave Background
- Summarize standard Big Bang timeline milestones
