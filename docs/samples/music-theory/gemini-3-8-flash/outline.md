# Working Music Theory: Practical Harmony and Song Analysis

_Depth: working_

A 4-week, step-by-step practical music theory course designed for solo learners. Focuses on building scales, decoding intervals and chords, analyzing song progressions with Roman numerals, and transposing music for real-world performance and composition.

## Week 1: Pitches, Rhythm, and the Major Scale Architecture

**Day 1: The 12 Pitches, Semitones, and Whole Steps**  
Topics: `pitch`, `chromatic-scale`, `intervals`

- Identify the 12 chromatic pitches and their enharmonic equivalents on a keyboard layout
- Calculate intervals of whole steps and half steps between specified note pairs

**Day 2: Rhythm Values, Meters, and Time Signatures**  
Topics: `rhythm`, `meter`, `time-signatures`

- Calculate beat values for combinations of whole, half, quarter, eighth, and sixteenth notes
- Identify measure groupings in standard 4/4, 3/4, and 6/8 time signatures

**Day 3: The Major Scale Formula**  
Topics: `major-scale`, `scale-construction`

- Spell any major scale using the whole-step and half-step pattern (W-W-H-W-W-W-H)
- Write out scale degree numbers and note names for C, G, D, and F major

**Day 4: Key Signatures and the Circle of Fifths**  
Topics: `circle-of-fifths`, `key-signatures`

- Determine the number of sharps or flats in any key signature using the Circle of Fifths
- Identify the root key from standard treble and bass clef key signatures

**Day 5: Intervals Part 1: Seconds, Thirds, and Octaves**  
Topics: `intervals`, `interval-construction`

- Calculate interval size and quality for major, minor, and perfect seconds and thirds
- Spell the correct note a major or minor third above a given root note

## Week 2: Intervals, Minor Keys, and Triad Construction

**Day 6: Intervals Part 2: Fourths, Fifths, Sixths, and Sevenths**  
Topics: `intervals`, `interval-inversion`

- Classify perfect fourths, perfect fifths, tritones, and major/minor sixths and sevenths
- Invert any simple interval and name the resulting interval size and quality

**Day 7: The Natural Minor Scale and Relative Keys**  
Topics: `minor-scale`, `relative-minor`

- Derive the natural minor scale using its relative major counterpart
- Spell natural minor scales using the interval formula (W-H-W-W-H-W-W)

**Day 8: Harmonic and Melodic Minor Scales**  
Topics: `minor-scale`, `leading-tone`

- Construct harmonic and melodic minor scales for any given root
- Explain why the 7th degree is raised in minor key compositions to create a leading tone

**Day 9: Building Major and Minor Triads**  
Topics: `triads`, `chord-construction`

- Construct major and minor triads using root, third, and fifth intervals
- Spell triad chords from standard lead sheet chord symbols (e.g., C, Am, F#m, Eb)

**Day 10: Diminished Triads, Augmented Triads, and Inversions**  
Topics: `triads`, `chord-inversions`

- Build diminished and augmented triads using stacked intervals
- Identify root position, first inversion, and second inversion slash chords

## Week 3: Diatonic Harmony, Roman Numerals, and Seventh Chords

**Day 11: Diatonic Triads in Major Keys**  
Topics: `diatonic-harmony`, `roman-numerals`

- Assign Roman numerals (I through vii°) to all seven diatonic triads in any major key
- Identify whether each scale degree triad in a major key is major, minor, or diminished

**Day 12: Standard Chord Progressions and Cadences**  
Topics: `chord-progressions`, `cadences`, `diatonic-harmony`

- Analyze popular chord progressions using Roman numerals (e.g., I-V-vi-IV and ii-V-I)
- Identify authentic, plagal, half, and deceptive cadences in short musical excerpts

**Day 13: Diatonic Triads in Minor Keys**  
Topics: `minor-scale`, `diatonic-harmony`, `chord-progressions`

- Construct diatonic triads for natural and harmonic minor scales using Roman numerals
- Explain why the minor key V chord is commonly altered to a major triad

**Day 14: Seventh Chords: Major 7, Dominant 7, and Minor 7**  
Topics: `seventh-chords`, `chord-construction`

- Construct Major 7th, Dominant 7th, Minor 7th, and Half-Diminished 7th chords
- Translate seventh chord lead sheet symbols (e.g., Cmaj7, G7, Am7, Bm7b5) into note spellings

**Day 15: Voice Leading Principles and Reading Lead Sheets**  
Topics: `voice-leading`, `lead-sheets`

- Arrange triad transitions using voice leading rules that minimize note movement
- Read and interpret a single-page lead sheet containing melody and chord symbols

## Week 4: Practical Analysis, Modulations, and Modes

**Day 16: Transposition Techniques**  
Topics: `transposition`, `roman-numerals`

- Transpose a chord progression to a new key using Roman numeral analysis
- Transpose a melodic line to accommodate a different vocal or instrumental range

**Day 17: Secondary Dominants**  
Topics: `secondary-dominants`, `chord-progressions`

- Identify secondary dominant chords (such as V/V and V/vi) within chord charts
- Write a secondary dominant chord that targets a specified diatonic destination chord

**Day 18: Borrowed Chords and Modal Interchange**  
Topics: `borrowed-chords`, `chord-progressions`

- Identify chords borrowed from the parallel minor in major-key songs (e.g., minor iv, bVI)
- Explain the emotional contrast produced by borrowed chords in modern songwriting

**Day 19: Everyday Modes: Dorian, Mixolydian, and Aeolian**  
Topics: `modes`, `scale-construction`

- Differentiate Dorian and Mixolydian modes from standard major and natural minor scales
- Identify characteristic modal scale degrees and modal chord vamp patterns

**Day 20: Full Song Harmonic Analysis**  
Topics: `harmonic-analysis`, `lead-sheets`, `roman-numerals`

- Perform an end-to-end Roman numeral analysis on a complete pop or film song chart
- Identify tonic key centers, borrowed chords, voice leading shifts, and cadence points
