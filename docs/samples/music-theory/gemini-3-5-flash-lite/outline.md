# Practical Music Theory for Everyday Musicians

_Depth: working_

A 4-week practical guide to music theory designed for independent learners, focusing on rhythm, scales, chords, and song structure.

## Week 1: Foundations of Sound, Pitch, and Rhythm

**Day 1: The Musical Alphabet and Piano Keyboard Layout**  
Topics: `musical-alphabet`, `piano-layout`

- Identify natural and accidental notes on a piano keyboard
- Locate any given note name across two octaves

**Day 2: Whole Steps, Half Steps, and Accidentals**  
Topics: `half-steps`, `accidentals`, `enharmonics`

- Calculate half steps and whole steps between any two adjacent keys
- Explain the difference between sharps and flats using enharmonic equivalents

**Day 3: Beats, Tempos, and Time Signatures**  
Topics: `tempo`, `time-signatures`

- Count steady beats at a specified tempo in beats per minute
- Interpret 4/4 and 3/4 time signatures in terms of beats per measure

**Day 4: Note Durations and Basic Rhythms**  
Topics: `note-durations`, `rhythm`

- Calculate the total duration of combined whole, half, quarter, and eighth notes
- Tap out a simple syncopated rhythm using quarter and eighth notes

**Day 5: Measures, Barlines, and Rest Values**  
Topics: `measures`, `rests`, `rhythm`

- Organize a sequence of notes and rests into correct measures for 4/4 time
- Count and clap a rhythm containing rests accurately

## Week 2: Scales and Key Signatures

**Day 6: The Major Scale Formula**  
Topics: `major-scale`, `scale-formulas`

- Apply the whole-step and half-step formula to construct any major scale
- Sing or play the C major scale from root to root

**Day 7: Key Signatures and the Circle of Fifths**  
Topics: `key-signatures`, `circle-of-fifths`

- Identify the number of sharps or flats in common major keys
- Use the circle of fifths to determine the relative keys for major scales

**Day 8: Natural Minor Scales**  
Topics: `natural-minor`, `scale-formulas`

- Apply the natural minor scale formula using whole and half steps
- Derive the relative minor scale from any given major scale

**Day 9: Harmonic and Melodic Minor Scales**  
Topics: `harmonic-minor`, `melodic-minor`

- Modify a natural minor scale to create the harmonic minor scale
- Explain why the melodic minor scale differs when ascending versus descending

**Day 10: Pentatonic Scales and Blues Scale Basics**  
Topics: `pentatonic-scale`, `blues-scale`

- Construct major and minor pentatonic scales from root notes
- Add the blues note to create a basic blues scale

## Week 3: Intervals and Chord Construction

**Day 11: Measuring Intervals by Quality and Number**  
Topics: `intervals`, `interval-quality`

- Calculate numeric intervals and qualities like major, minor, and perfect
- Identify intervals up to an octave by sight and sound

**Day 12: Building Major and Minor Triads**  
Topics: `triads`, `major-scale`, `intervals`

- Stack intervals to build major and minor triads from any root note
- Differentiate between major and minor chord sounds

**Day 13: Diminished and Augmented Triads**  
Topics: `diminished-triads`, `augmented-triads`, `triads`

- Construct diminished and augmented triads using stack intervals
- Explain the psychological tension created by diminished and augmented chords

**Day 14: Seventh Chords: Major 7, Dominant 7, and Minor 7**  
Topics: `seventh-chords`, `triads`

- Construct dominant 7th, major 7th, and minor 7th chords from root notes
- Identify the specific interval formulas that define each seventh chord type

**Day 15: Chord Inversions**  
Topics: `chord-inversions`, `triads`

- Rearrange root-position triads into first and second inversions
- Determine the bass note of an inverted chord from chord symbols

## Week 4: Harmonization and Song Structure

**Day 16: Harmonizing the Major Scale**  
Topics: `diatonic-chords`, `major-scale`

- Derive the diatonic chords of a major scale key
- Classify each diatonic chord as major, minor, or diminished

**Day 17: Common Chord Progressions**  
Topics: `chord-progressions`, `roman-numerals`

- Roman numeral analysis of the I-IV-V-I and ii-V-I progressions
- Transpose a standard chord progression into a new key

**Day 18: Cadences and Musical Phrasing**  
Topics: `cadences`, `chord-progressions`

- Identify authentic and plagal cadences at the end of musical phrases
- Explain how cadences create punctuation in a song structure

**Day 19: Song Form and Arrangement Basics**  
Topics: `song-form`, `arrangement`

- Map out standard song forms like verse-chorus-bridge
- Analyze how dynamic changes affect the emotional arc of an arrangement

**Day 20: Putting It All Together: Analyzing a Complete Song**  
Topics: `song-analysis`, `key-signatures`, `chord-progressions`

- Analyze the key, time signature, and chord progression of a provided lead sheet
- Explain how theory concepts learned in the course apply to practical performance
