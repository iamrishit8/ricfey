# Mastering Personal Finance in India

_Depth: working_

A practical 4-week guide for adults in India to take control of their income, expenses, investments, and taxes with confidence.

## Week 1: Cash Flow and Budgeting Basics

**Day 1: Mapping Your Net Worth**  
Topics: `net-worth`, `assets-liabilities`

- List all personal assets and liabilities
- Calculate total net worth in Indian Rupees

**Day 2: Tracking Income and Fixed Expenses**  
Topics: `income-tracking`, `fixed-expenses`

- Categorize monthly income sources
- Identify and list all fixed monthly expenses

**Day 3: Controlling Variable Expenses**  
Topics: `variable-expenses`, `spending-caps`

- Analyze discretionary spending habits
- Set realistic monthly caps for variable categories

**Day 4: Applying the 50/30/20 Rule**  
Topics: `budgeting-rule`, `net-worth`

- Allocate income into needs, wants, and savings using the 50/30/20 framework

**Day 5: Building an Emergency Fund Target**  
Topics: `emergency-fund`, `liquidity`

- Calculate the required size of a 6-month emergency fund
- Select safe, liquid parking spaces for emergency cash

## Week 2: Debt Management and Banking Essentials

**Day 6: Evaluating Good Debt vs Bad Debt**  
Topics: `debt-types`, `loans`

- Differentiate between wealth-building and depreciating consumer debt

**Day 7: Credit Cards and CIBIL Scores**  
Topics: `credit-score`, `credit-cards`

- Explain how CIBIL scores are calculated
- List actions to maintain a credit score above 750

**Day 8: Choosing Debt Payoff Strategies**  
Topics: `debt-payoff`, `loans`

- Compare the debt avalanche and debt snowball methods
- Choose a payoff strategy for existing liabilities

**Day 9: Optimizing Bank Accounts**  
Topics: `bank-accounts`, `fees`

- Select appropriate savings and current accounts based on interest and fees
- Identify hidden banking charges

**Day 10: Understanding Digital Payments Security**  
Topics: `digital-security`, `upi`

- Apply safe practices for UPI and net banking
- Identify common financial scams in India

**Day 11: Beating Inflation with Asset Allocation**

**Day 12: Fixed Income: FDs, RDs, and EPF**

**Day 14: Setting Up a SIP**

**Day 15: Basics of Direct Equity**
