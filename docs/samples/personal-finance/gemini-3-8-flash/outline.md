# Working Personal Finance for Adults in India

_Depth: working_

A 4-week, hands-on personal finance course designed to build practical financial self-sufficiency. Learn to track cash flow, eliminate debt, secure appropriate insurance, optimize taxes, and build a resilient long-term investment portfolio using standard Indian instruments.

## Week 1: Cash Flow, Budgeting, and Debt Elimination

**Day 1: Auditing Cash Flow and Net Worth**  
Topics: `net-worth`, `cash-flow`, `budgeting`

- List all personal assets and liabilities to calculate total net worth in rupees
- Categorize bank and UPI transactions from the past 30 days into fixed needs, wants, and savings

**Day 2: Designing a Zero-Based Monthly Budget**  
Topics: `budgeting`, `cash-flow`

- Allocate monthly take-home income using a modified 50/30/20 framework tailored to local living costs
- Identify at least three non-essential recurring expenses that can be negotiated or eliminated

**Day 3: Constructing an Emergency Fund**  
Topics: `emergency-fund`, `liquidity`, `banking`

- Calculate personal mandatory monthly survival expenses for a 6-month buffer
- Select an appropriate split between high-liquidity savings accounts and liquid mutual funds or sweep-in fixed deposits

**Day 4: Deconstructing High-Cost Debt**  
Topics: `debt-management`, `credit-cards`

- Calculate the effective annualized interest rate of credit card revolving debt and personal loans
- Explain why paying only the minimum amount due on a credit card triggers long-term debt accumulation

**Day 5: Executing Debt Payoff Accelerators**  
Topics: `debt-management`, `interest-calculation`

- Draft a side-by-side repayment schedule comparing the debt avalanche and debt snowball strategies
- Select the optimal payoff strategy for a given set of consumer debts and establish accelerated payment milestones

## Week 2: Risk Protection, Credit Health, and Taxes

**Day 6: Procuring Pure Term Life Insurance**  
Topics: `insurance`, `term-insurance`, `risk-management`

- Calculate required life insurance cover using human life value and liabilities minus liquid assets
- Identify red-flag policy features to avoid, including investment-linked endowments and ULIPs

**Day 7: Selecting Comprehensive Health Insurance**  
Topics: `insurance`, `health-insurance`, `risk-management`

- Evaluate health insurance policies based on room rent limits, copay clauses, and restoration benefits
- Structure an optimal base cover plus super top-up policy combination to minimize premium costs

**Day 8: Managing Credit Score and Banking Security**  
Topics: `credit-score`, `banking`

- Interpret a credit information bureau report (CIBIL/Experian) to identify factors harming the score
- Implement concrete measures to keep credit utilization ratio below 30 percent and enable transaction-level security

**Day 9: Navigating Old vs. New Income Tax Regimes**  
Topics: `tax-planning`, `income-tax`

- Calculate annual tax liability under both the Old and New Income Tax regimes for a salaried individual
- Determine which tax regime provides a lower effective tax rate based on eligible deductions

**Day 10: Optimizing Statutory Tax Deductions**  
Topics: `tax-planning`, `fixed-income`, `retirement-planning`

- Compare Section 80C instruments including EPF, PPF, and ELSS on lock-in periods and post-tax returns
- Evaluate additional tax-advantaged vehicles such as National Pension System (NPS) under Section 80CCD

## Week 3: Investment Foundations and Asset Allocation

**Day 11: Inflation and Real Returns**  
Topics: `inflation`, `compounding`

- Calculate the real, post-inflation purchasing power of a lump sum over 10 and 20 years
- Demonstrate using the Rule of 72 how inflation halves capital without active compounding

**Day 12: Fixed Income and Capital Preservation**  
Topics: `fixed-income`, `sovereign-gold-bonds`

- Compare the risk, liquidity, and post-tax yield of fixed deposits, PPF, and Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs)
- Calculate post-tax yield on debt instruments based on marginal income tax slabs

**Day 13: Demystifying Mutual Funds and Indexing**  
Topics: `mutual-funds`, `index-funds`

- Differentiate between active equity mutual funds and broad-market index funds (Nifty 50, Nifty Next 50)
- Calculate the long-term wealth impact of total expense ratio (TER) differences between regular and direct plans

**Day 14: Automating Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs)**  
Topics: `mutual-funds`, `sip`, `investing-mechanics`

- Calculate unit accumulation and average purchase price during market drawdowns via rupee cost averaging
- Set up an automated monthly SIP mandate aligned with a designated salary credit date

**Day 15: Constructing an Asset Allocation Framework**  
Topics: `asset-allocation`, `portfolio-construction`

- Determine a personal equity-to-debt ratio matching investment horizon and risk tolerance
- Construct a diversified starter portfolio divided across broad-market index funds, debt, and liquid instruments

## Week 4: Goal Planning, Maintenance, and Financial Hygiene

**Day 16: Reverse-Engineering Time-Bound Goals**  
Topics: `goal-planning`, `asset-allocation`

- Calculate the required monthly SIP contribution to fund a defined milestone using future value formulas
- Categorize financial goals into short-term, medium-term, and long-term buckets with appropriate asset classes

**Day 17: Calculating the Retirement Independence Corpus**  
Topics: `retirement-planning`, `goal-planning`

- Calculate targeted retirement corpus using expected annual post-retirement expenses and withdrawal rates
- Model the cumulative contribution of compulsory employer retirement savings (EPF/Gratuity) toward the retirement target

**Day 18: Navigating Capital Gains and Withdrawal Taxes**  
Topics: `tax-planning`, `capital-gains`, `investing-mechanics`

- Calculate short-term and long-term capital gains tax on equity mutual funds after the annual exempt threshold
- Formulate a tax-efficient withdrawal sequence across equity, debt, and liquid reserves

**Day 19: Executing Annual Portfolio Rebalancing**  
Topics: `asset-allocation`, `portfolio-construction`

- Identify asset allocation drift using a 5 percent tolerance band threshold
- Formulate rebalancing transactions that realign target weights while minimizing unnecessary capital gains realization

**Day 20: Setting Up Legal Nominations and Financial Automation**  
Topics: `estate-planning`, `banking`, `financial-hygiene`

- Audit nomination and joint-holding statuses across all bank accounts, mutual funds, EPF, and insurance policies
- Draft a personal master financial inventory document to ensure household continuity in emergencies
