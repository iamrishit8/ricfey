# Practical Personal Finance for Working Adults in India

_Depth: working_

A 4-week, step-by-step program to gain operational control over everyday personal finances. Move from budgeting and debt cleanup to insurance safeguards, tax optimization, and long-term goal-based investing using standard Indian financial instruments.

## Week 1: Cash Flow, Debt Liquidation, and Banking Foundation

**Day 1: Cash Flow Audit and Baseline Budgeting**  
Topics: `budgeting`, `cash-flow`, `bank-statements`

- Calculate accurate monthly take-home income across all regular and irregular sources
- Categorize bank statement transactions into essentials, discretionary lifestyle expenses, and savings using the 50/30/20 framework
- Identify recurring subscription leaks and discretionary spending anomalies in 60 days of transaction data

**Day 2: Optimizing Bank Accounts and Sweep Deposits**  
Topics: `banking`, `cash-flow`, `interest-rates`

- Configure a multi-account banking structure separating salary inflow, household spending, and savings
- Compare standard savings account interest against auto-sweep fixed deposit mechanisms
- Calculate net yield differences after taking Section 80TTA limits into consideration

**Day 3: Credit Score Mechanics and Credit Card Utilization**  
Topics: `credit-score`, `credit-cards`, `debt-management`

- Inspect an official CIBIL credit report to spot inaccuracies, unauthorized inquiries, or reporting errors
- Calculate credit utilization ratios across individual credit cards to stay strictly below the 30% threshold
- Establish automated payment schedules to clear statement balances in full and eliminate interest compounding

**Day 4: Structured Debt Elimination Strategies**  
Topics: `debt-management`, `interest-rates`

- List all existing personal loans, EMIs, and outstanding card dues ordered by annual percentage rate (APR)
- Compare total interest payout between Debt Avalanche and Debt Snowball repayment strategies
- Calculate the accelerated repayment timeline achieved by applying an extra lump sum to high-cost debt

**Day 5: Building an Automated Monthly Operating Budget**  
Topics: `budgeting`, `automation`, `cash-flow`

- Draft an end-to-end monthly zero-based budget reflecting current income and revised debt payoffs
- Schedule automatic standing instructions (SI) on salary day to automate savings before discretionary spending
- Establish a monthly ledger reconciliation routine that takes under 15 minutes to run

## Week 2: Risk Mitigation: Emergency Reserves and Insurance

**Day 6: Sizing and Stashing the Emergency Fund**  
Topics: `emergency-fund`, `liquidity`, `banking`

- Calculate precise emergency fund target amounts equal to 6 months of non-negotiable living expenses and EMIs
- Allocate the emergency reserve across liquid mutual funds, high-yield savings, and short-term fixed deposits
- Draft clear non-negotiable ground rules defining what constitutes a financial emergency

**Day 7: Health Insurance Architecture**  
Topics: `health-insurance`, `risk-management`

- Evaluate health insurance policy Wordings for room rent sub-limits, co-pay clauses, and disease waiting periods
- Compare cost-efficiency of high base health covers against base covers paired with super top-up plans
- Calculate appropriate aggregate health coverage based on family size and metro healthcare costs

**Day 8: Pure Term Life Insurance Planning**  
Topics: `life-insurance`, `risk-management`

- Calculate required life cover sum assured using the Human Life Value (HLV) and expense-replacement methods
- Contrast pure term insurance policies with ULIPs, endowment, and money-back products regarding net cost and returns
- Select policy tenure matching retirement horizon rather than whole-of-life options

**Day 9: Personal Accident and Critical Illness Protection**  
Topics: `health-insurance`, `risk-management`

- Assess policy exclusions and benefit triggers in standalone Personal Accident and Permanent Disability covers
- Calculate optimal disability cover multiples relative to annual gross income
- Determine whether a standalone Critical Illness policy or a health policy rider provides better claims terms

**Day 10: Insurance Portfolio Audit and Claim Readiness**  
Topics: `risk-management`, `health-insurance`, `life-insurance`

- Audit existing family insurance policies to identify overlapping covers and surrender-worthy endowment plans
- Compile a portable claim dossier containing cashless cards, policy schedules, and third-party administrator (TPA) contacts
- Draft an emergency instructions document for family members detailing claim initiation procedures

## Week 3: Wealth Accumulation: Core Investment Instruments

**Day 11: Inflation Realities and Compounding Principles**  
Topics: `investing-basics`, `inflation`, `compound-interest`

- Calculate the real rate of return on traditional deposits after subtracting retail CPI inflation and personal taxes
- Calculate long-term final corpus values of systematic investment plans (SIP) using compound growth models
- Explain why holding surplus capital entirely in fixed bank deposits guarantees loss of purchasing power

**Day 12: Government-Backed Debt Vehicles: EPF, PPF, and SSY**  
Topics: `fixed-income`, `government-schemes`, `retirement`

- Compare lock-ins, tax status (EEE vs. EET), and historical returns across EPF, VPF, PPF, and Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana
- Audit an EPFO unified portal passbook for missing employer contributions and interest credits
- Determine the optimal annual allocation to Public Provident Fund (PPF) for risk-free compounding

**Day 13: Equity Mutual Funds and Index Investing**  
Topics: `mutual-funds`, `equity-investing`, `index-funds`

- Distinguish between passive index funds (Nifty 50, Nifty Next 50) and active mutual funds
- Compare Direct plans versus Regular mutual fund plans to calculate total expense ratio (TER) savings over 20 years
- Screen mutual funds for tracking error, fund manager tenure, and asset under management (AUM) stability

**Day 14: Asset Allocation and Risk Profiling**  
Topics: `asset-allocation`, `portfolio-management`, `equity-investing`

- Determine a personal equity-to-debt asset allocation based on investment horizon and drawdown tolerance
- Construct a diversified 3-fund core portfolio using broad index funds, fixed income, and international equity exposure
- Define fixed mathematical rebalancing bands to trim or add assets when allocations drift by over 5%

**Day 15: National Pension System (NPS) Mechanics**  
Topics: `retirement`, `nps`, `tax-planning`

- Compare Tier 1 and Tier 2 account features, withdrawal restrictions, and mandatory annuity rules
- Select equity, corporate debt, and government bond allocation splits using Active Choice versus Auto Choice
- Calculate tax deduction benefits available under Section 80CCD(1B) and employer corporate NPS contributions

## Week 4: Taxes, Goal Mapping, and Ongoing Governance

**Day 16: Income Tax Regimes Optimization**  
Topics: `tax-planning`, `income-tax`

- Calculate total tax liability under the Old Tax Regime with itemized deductions (HRA, 80C, 80D)
- Calculate total tax liability under the New Tax Regime using simplified concessional slabs
- Select the optimal tax regime for the financial year based on verifiable deductions and salary structure

**Day 17: Capital Gains Taxation and Tax-Harvesting**  
Topics: `tax-planning`, `capital-gains`, `mutual-funds`

- Calculate short-term (STCG) and long-term (LTCG) capital gains tax across equity and non-equity investments
- Execute annual tax-loss or tax-gain harvesting within the exemption limit for equity mutual funds
- Differentiate the taxation rules governing listed bonds, debt mutual funds, and fixed deposits

**Day 18: Goal-Based Investment Mapping**  
Topics: `goal-planning`, `portfolio-management`, `mutual-funds`

- Categorize personal life goals into short-term (under 3 years), medium-term (3-7 years), and long-term horizons
- Map financial instruments to specific goal buckets based on capital preservation vs. growth needs
- Calculate the required step-up SIP contribution per month to reach target figures for specific timed goals

**Day 19: Estate Readiness: Nominations and Basic Wills**  
Topics: `estate-planning`, `legal-documentation`

- Verify nominee designations across all bank accounts, demat portals, EPF, and insurance policies
- Differentiate legal rights between a registered nominee and a legal heir under Indian succession frameworks
- Draft a valid, handwritten holographic basic Will with required witness signature clauses

**Day 20: The Annual Financial Review and Net Worth Tracking**  
Topics: `net-worth`, `portfolio-management`, `budgeting`

- Construct a personal net worth statement consolidating current liquid assets, investments, properties, and debts
- Execute a systematic portfolio rebalancing action to restore target equity-debt weights
- Create an annual personal finance maintenance calendar covering tax filing, policy renewals, and rebalancing
